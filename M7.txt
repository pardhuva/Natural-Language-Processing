
//morphology 
 
  Morphology = study of the internal structure of words.

//intro 
  Morphology studies how words are formed
  Words are built using smaller meaningful units called morphemes.
  ex: revisited 
       re-again , visit - action , ed- past tense 
  morphology = rules + building blocks of words 

what is morpheme ?
  A morpheme is the smallest meaningful unit. 
   that cannot be broken further for a meaningful unit 
   not the same as :
    syllable (sound-based)
    word (can have multiple morphemes)

    ex: tuxedo (1 morpheme, 3 syllables)
        sixths (3 morphemes, 1 syllable)

what is syllable ?
  a syllable is 
    . a unit of sound 
    . contains one vowel sound 
    . spoken in one breath 

  syllable = 
    Onset (O) - consonants before vowel 
    Nucleus (N) - vowel 
    Coda (C) - consonants after vowel 
    Nucleus + coda = rhyme 

   ex: Out : none- onset  , o - nucleus ut - coda 
       pond : p-onset ,o - nucleus , nd - coda 
    
    two ways to classify syllables 
    1) number of syllables :
       . Monosyllable - 1 syllable 
       . polysyllable - more than 1 
    2) ending type 
       . open syllable - ends with vowel 
       . closed syllable - ends with consonant 
    
//open syllable 
  Ends with vowel
  Vowel sound is long
  ex: to, hi, go, he , she 

//closed syllable 
  Ends with consonant
  Vowel sound is short
  ex: cat,flat, fast 

//sounds : vowel types 
 Monophthongs - single vowel sound 
 Diphthongs - two vowel sound combined 

 //monophthongs  
  A monophthong is where there is one vowel sound in a syllable.
  ex: fat,thin,hot 

 //diphthongs 
  two vowel sounds glide together.
  ex: crazy,boat,now....

//Morpheme types 
 two main groups 
 1. Free Morpheme  - stands alone 
   . Lexical 
   . Functional 
 2. Bound Morpheme  - cannot stand alone 
   . Derivational 
   . Inflectional 

//Free morphemes 
 Lexical (open class)
  . nouns,verbs,adjectives,adverbs 
  . (book,love,sing)
 Functional (closed class)
  . pronouns, articles, conjunctions 
  . (the,and,in)

//Bound morphmes (Affixation)
 types of affixes:
  prefix - front (re-order)
  suffix - end (order-ed)
  infix - inserted inside word 

//inflectional morphology 
 inflection - adds grammatical info , doesnt change world class. 
 ex: 
  play - played 
  laptop - laptops 

// inflectional morphemes 
  Nouns . s,
   verbs - s,ed,en,ing 
   adjectives - er,est 

//derivational morphology 
  Derivation = creates a new word, often changes word class.
  ex: 
   alchohol - alchoholic 
   clear - clearance 
   active - activity 
   read - readable 

 common examples - ic,ly,ate,ity,ship,re
