 Functional approach (Jakobson’s 6 functions)

NLP-12[2]

Language performs 6 functions:

Addressor → who is speaking
Addressee → who is receiving
Message → what is being said
Context → situation
Code → language used (English, Telugu)
Contact → channel (phone, face-to-face)
