
Phonetics ,Phonology 
 phonetics is the study of human sounds 
 phonology is the classification of sounds within system of a particular language or languages 
 three types : 
  production(articulatory)
  transmission(acoustic)
  perception(auditive) of sounds 

phones vs phonemes vs allophones 
phones - basic sound units 
  ex: [k],[b] [u]
phonemes - smallest sound units that can distinguish meaning in a sentence 
  exL /p/,/b/
allophones - variants of a phoneme 
   ex: [ph] in "pin" vs [p] -> "spin"

 types of phonemes 
  1. production(articulatory) - how sounds are produced (speech)
  2. transmission(acoustic) - how sounds travel adn their physical properties 
  3. perception(auditory) - how sounds are perceiveed by the ear 

Phonetics = physical and perceptual aspects of sounds.
Phonology = functional role of sounds in a language.
Phones and allophones are phonetic, phonemes are phonological.

places of articualtion - where sounds are made 
 lips - p,b 
 teeth - theeta,delta 
 alveoral - t,d,s 
 hard palate , soft palate 

 manners of articulation - how sounds are made 
 how airflow is shaped or blocked when producing sound 
 stop - p,t,k 
 nasal - m,n,
 lateral - I 
 trill,tap
 
  Articulation in linguistics is about how speech sounds are produced using vocal organs like tongue, lips, nose, teeth, and vocal cords.
